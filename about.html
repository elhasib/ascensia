Our story

Why we built this.

The members of this team applied to top universities globally. We know what that process looks like without a counselor, without money for consultants, and without a single person in your school who had done it before. The information existed. The financial aid existed. The opportunity was there. What was missing was access to the network and knowledge that makes it possible to actually act on any of it.

We believe that university admissions system in most of the world distributes outcomes that track closely with socioeconomic advantage. It is a structural problem, and it is one that a well-designed program can meaningfully push back against, one cohort at a time.
